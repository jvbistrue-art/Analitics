If you put less pressure on the piano, it will give you a softer sound. Now you can do something more complex with the sound, which will help you create longer musical phrases. So these two signs are very important in music. One of them is called crescendo. Crescendo in Italian means gradually louder. So it's not an immediately loud sound — you have to start on the softest point and then gradually increase the volume by pressing the keys harder and harder and harder. Now this sign is called diminuendo. In Italian it means gradually softer. So you will start on the loudest point here, and then you gradually decrease the volume by pressing it less and less and less and less hard. So you put less pressure gradually. So, to make a long phrase, you have to build a crescendo and then a diminuendo. But sometimes you can see both of these signs — not together, just one sign at a time. So you will have just a crescendo, or you can have just a diminuendo. All you need to know is to recognize the signs and know what they mean.
