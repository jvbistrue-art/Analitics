Now, let's talk about the fourth and the fifth. So we are going to follow the same logic: you have to think about the English alphabet. So if you have four letter names in alphabetical order involved in an interval, it will be called a fourth. Now let's look at the example. So we have A, and let's count: A, B, C, D. If you put the sounds one after another, like A and D — so we have A, and you have D, one after another — you write them down one after another, and you press them on the piano one after another. They will be called melodic intervals, because they create a melody. Now the same you do with the fifth, A–E. So A and E will create a melody, a fifth.
