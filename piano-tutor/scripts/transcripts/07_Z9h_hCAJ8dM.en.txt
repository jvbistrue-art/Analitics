Now I'm going to start teaching you some theory, and we are going to start with intervals. An interval in music is the distance between sounds. So the distance, as you know, can be a short distance or it can be a long distance, and it's all between sounds. These different distances are called intervals, and they all have different names. The two intervals we are going to talk about right now are a second and a third. When you have two sounds next to each other in alphabetical order — like A and B, B and C, C and D, something like this — C D, or you can have A B, or you can have F G — these are called seconds, because you have two notes next to each other in alphabetical order, two letter names next to each other in alphabetical order. So you have line-space, and you can have space-line. When you think about the intervals, you need to think about the letters of the alphabet in order, so as not to get confused.
