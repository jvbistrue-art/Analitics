Okay, guys, now it's time to learn chords and talk about chords. What is a chord? A chord is a combination of more than two sounds pressed together. The difference between an interval and a chord is the number of sounds. The first chord we are going to learn is a C major chord. A C major chord is three notes pressed together — C, E, G. So we can have the notes in the treble clef: C, E, G. So you press them together — C, E, G — a C major chord. You can have the same C major chord in the bass clef. In the bass clef, remember, the notes are written in a different place, so the C for the bass clef will be here: C, E, G. C, E, G. Now let's practice our C major chords. So first we will play them in their place, with your left hand; then we will play the same chord with your right hand in the treble clef; and then you will play with both hands. So let's start. Put your hands in C position. Right now you don't need your right hand — just put your left hand in the C position, finger five on C below middle C. So five, three, one — C, E, G.
